About the AI-powered wildlife recommendation system

This project addresses the challenge of helping tourists and wildlife enthusiasts identify the most suitable Ugandan national park and environmental conditions for viewing specific wildlife.

By learning from historical tourism and wildlife observation data, the model identifies patterns between weather, season, and sightings. It then recommends the wildlife most likely to be found and the park best matched to the selected conditions.

The web application delivers that intelligence through a simple, responsive interface so visitors can plan smarter trips with confidence.

Project goals

  • Support informed safari planning
  • Improve wildlife viewing success
  • Promote Uganda’s national parks
