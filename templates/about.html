About Lambula

AI-Powered Wildlife Explorer for Uganda

Lambula is an intelligent wildlife recommendation and prediction system designed to help tourists, travelers, and safari planners discover the best wildlife experiences in Uganda.

Using machine learning and historical environmental data, Lambula predicts the most likely wildlife you can encounter based on weather conditions and suggests the best parks to visit.

Conservation Focused

We support wildlife conservation by promoting responsible and informed tourism.

Simple, fast and intuitive interface for everyone from tourists to tour operators.
